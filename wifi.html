RJ WiFi Manager

Welcome to RJ's ESP32 WiFi Configuration.
Connect your ESP32 to your home WiFi.

How to connect:
1. Connect to RJ's ESP32 WiFi.
2. Open http://192.168.4.1.
3. Enter your home WiFi name.
4. Enter your home WiFi password.
5. Click Save & Connect.
6. Wait for ESP32 to restart.
7. Check Serial Monitor for the new IP address.
8. Open that IP address in Chrome.
